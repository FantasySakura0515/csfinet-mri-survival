# Dataset terms and project license

Checked on 17 September 2026. These notes document the public source statements consulted; they do not grant dataset rights or establish permission beyond the rights held by each provider.

The project applies Apache 2.0 to contributor-owned code, documentation, configurations and its six trained checkpoint files. MRI volumes and clinical metadata are excluded from the repository and weight archives. The project license does not replace data-access agreements, privacy rights, dataset citation obligations or third-party licenses.

## Sources checked

| Source | Observed statement |
| --- | --- |
| [Kaggle: BraTS2020 Training Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/awsaf49/brats2020-training-data) | The public dataset API reports `CC0: Public Domain`. The study uses version 3 for source metadata. |
| [Kaggle: BraTS20 Training and Validation](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/awsaf49/brats20-dataset-training-validation) | The public dataset API reports `CC0: Public Domain`. The study uses version 1 for native NIfTI volumes. |
| [Original BraTS 2020 data page](https://www.med.upenn.edu/cbica/brats2020/data.html) | Describes research use and requires citation of three publications; additional data citations are requested where the publication venue permits them. |

A mirror's metadata does not establish that it can waive every original provider's rights or agreement. Neither a Kaggle CC0 label nor this project's Apache license should be interpreted as a blanket clearance of third-party rights for every downstream use. These sources do not expressly determine the licensing status of independently trained weights; the Apache grant is limited to the project contributors' rights in the released artifacts.

## BraTS citations

The original BraTS page identifies these three publications:

1. Menze et al. The Multimodal Brain Tumor Image Segmentation Benchmark (BRATS). *IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging*, 2015. [DOI: 10.1109/TMI.2014.2377694](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2014.2377694).
2. Bakas et al. Advancing The Cancer Genome Atlas glioma MRI collections with expert segmentation labels and radiomic features. *Scientific Data*, 2017. [DOI: 10.1038/sdata.2017.117](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2017.117).
3. Bakas et al. Identifying the Best Machine Learning Algorithms for Brain Tumor Segmentation, Progression Assessment, and Overall Survival Prediction in the BRATS Challenge. 2018. [arXiv:1811.02629](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.02629).

Additional data citations listed by the provider are [TCGA-GBM labels and features](https://doi.org/10.7937/K9/TCIA.2017.KLXWJJ1Q) and [TCGA-LGG labels and features](https://doi.org/10.7937/K9/TCIA.2017.GJQ7R0EF).
