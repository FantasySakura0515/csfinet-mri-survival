# Statistical reconciliation

The public segmentation CSVs and manuscript previously used different bootstrap seed schedules: a lower-level export advanced through four measures (Dice, precision, recall, IoU), whereas the manuscript report advanced through three (Dice, precision, recall), then through the TTA summaries. The published TTA export also restarted at the base seed. This changed Monte Carlo confidence-interval endpoints while leaving means and sample SDs unchanged.

The manuscript schedule was retained because it was the existing reporting source. All 36 Dice/precision/recall summaries were recomputed from the saved patient metrics with 10,000 BCa resamples and compared against that source; means, SDs and interval endpoints agree to 1e-12. All 20 paired summary rows were also recomputed: effects, intervals and available raw/Holm-adjusted p values agree to 1e-12. There was no model retraining, inference or result-based choice of seed. At four decimal places, the correction affects 11 Dice intervals across the public raw/TTA CSVs; additional precision/recall intervals have been synchronized too.

The canonical mapping uses base seed 20260914, lexicographically sorted patient IDs, CSFINet before U-Net, regions WT/TC/ET and measures Dice/precision/recall. Raw uses offsets 0–17; TTA uses 18–35. Diagnostic IoU retains the lower-level four-measure offset and cannot shift those seeds. The paired schedule starts at 20260964; its exact seeds are recorded in `segmentation-paired-tests.csv`. This offset is fixed, independent of the number of optional inference variants available locally.

The implementation is shared by the full evaluator and `scripts/summarize_results.py`. `results/reference/segmentation-statistics.json` records software versions, input hashes, interval seeds and aggregate output hashes. Individual patient metrics are generated locally from the separately obtained data and are not distributed. The corrected intervals do not change the manuscript's findings.
