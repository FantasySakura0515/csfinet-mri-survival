# Evaluation protocol

The study uses 235 patients, partitioned into 188 training and 47 held-out test cases. Within training, 150 patients are used for development training and 38 for validation-based selection. All modalities and slices of a patient remain in one group. Model and method choices are fixed before test evaluation. See [methods](methods.md) and [partition metadata](protocol.json). No preregistration or external validation is claimed.
